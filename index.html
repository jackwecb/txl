隐私政策

通讯录Duo · 发布者：Mr Jack · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日

通讯录Duo 是离线通讯录工具。App 本身不设账号、不接入广告或统计 SDK，也不主动上传联系人。

1. 使用的数据

您可以在 App 内创建独立的本地联系人。获得系统通讯录授权后，App 才能读取和管理授权范围内的系统联系人。姓名、电话号码及您填写的其他字段用于列表、搜索、编辑、重复项检查、导入导出、批量处理和您主动开启的生日提醒。拒绝或撤销系统通讯录权限后，App 内联系人仍可使用；系统联系人功能将受限。

2. 存储与传输

App 内联系人保存在设备上的 App 数据中；系统联系人保存在 iOS 通讯录中。App 不将联系人发送给发布者或第三方服务器。系统通讯录可能通过您设置的系统账户同步；设备备份由 iOS 和您的设置控制，这些行为不由本 App 关闭或管理。

您主动导出、分享、复制或保存文件时，数据会进入您选择的文件位置或接收 App。请自行确认接收方及其隐私规则。App 提供的加密备份由您设置密码并自行保管；忘记密码可能无法恢复。表格导出只涵盖模板支持的字段，不能替代完整备份。

3. 通知

只有您主动开启生日提醒并授予通知权限时，App 才会在本机创建提醒。提醒中可能显示联系人姓名。您可在 App 或 iOS 设置中关闭提醒及通知权限。

4. 保留、删除与权限

App 内联系人保留至您在 App 内删除、清除 App 数据或删除 App。删除 App 通常会移除 App 内数据，但不会因此删除系统通讯录中的联系人。系统联系人由 iOS、相关账户及您的操作管理；通过 App 执行的系统联系人删除会写入系统通讯录。您可在 iOS 设置中撤销通讯录或通知权限。请在删除或换机前自行导出需要保留的数据。

5. 本网页与联系

当您主动打开本政策等网页时，网页托管服务商可能处理访问所需的 IP 地址等技术日志；网页不接收 App 中的联系人。若您主动发送邮件，发布者会收到您提供的邮箱地址和邮件内容，仅用于回复及处理反馈。

6. 变更与咨询

本政策随 App 功能变化更新，并在此页面标注更新日期。隐私问题请联系 wahahaadd@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy

Contacts Duo · Publisher: Mr Jack · Updated October 29, 2026

Contacts Duo is an offline contacts utility. The app has no account, advertising, or analytics SDK and does not proactively upload contacts.

1. Data used

You may create contacts in the app's separate local address book. The app accesses system contacts only after you grant permission, and only within the authorized scope. Names, phone numbers, and other fields you enter are used for listing, search, editing, duplicate review, import and export, batch operations, and birthday reminders you choose to enable. If you deny or revoke Contacts permission, local app contacts remain available while system contact features are limited.

2. Storage and transfer

Local app contacts are stored in the app's data on your device. System contacts remain in iOS Contacts. The app does not send contacts to the publisher or third-party servers. System contacts may sync through accounts you configure, and device backups depend on iOS and your settings; the app does not control those services.

When you choose to export, share, copy, or save a file, its data goes to the location or recipient app you select. Please review that recipient's privacy practices. Encrypted backups use a password you set and keep; losing it may prevent recovery. Spreadsheet exports include only supported template fields and are not a complete backup.

3. Notifications

Birthday reminders are created on your device only after you enable them and grant notification permission. A reminder may display a contact's name. You can turn reminders off in the app or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings.

4. Retention, deletion, and permissions

Local app contacts remain until you delete them in the app, clear app data, or delete the app. Deleting the app normally removes its local data but does not itself delete system contacts. iOS, connected accounts, and your actions manage system contacts; a system contact deletion you perform in the app is written to iOS Contacts. You can revoke Contacts or Notifications permission in iOS Settings. Export data you wish to keep before deleting the app or changing devices.

5. This website and contact

If you choose to open these web pages, the hosting provider may process technical access logs such as your IP address. The site receives no contacts from the app. If you email us, the publisher receives the address and content you provide solely to respond and handle feedback.

6. Changes and questions

This policy may be updated as app features change; the date above will be updated. For privacy questions, email wahahaadd@gmail.com.

Contacts Duo · Publisher: Mr Jack · Updated October 29, 2026

Before a batch edit, import, merge, or deletion, review the preview, destination address book, and scope of the action.

1. Service

Contacts Duo provides on-device contact management, search, duplicate review, batch operations, import and export, and local reminders. You should have the right to process the contact information you import or edit, and review it before writing, exporting, or sharing it. Matching names or numbers are candidates for review, not proof that two records belong to the same person. System contacts and notifications depend on iOS permissions you grant. Denying permission does not prevent use of local app contacts. We aim to provide reliable functionality, but cannot guarantee against data loss caused by device failure, account changes, third-party files, or user error.
